# Mansion Island: where to explore

Our Minecraft Java 26.3 world uses seed **`4358921489363102297`**. You start on a small island beside a woodland mansion and village. The community [seed discovery](https://www.reddit.com/r/minecraftseeds/comments/1wt81vg/java_insane_spawn_island_woodland_mansion_crushes/) describes a lush cave and several underground structures nearby.

## More stops for the progression guide

These are **26.3 seed-map estimates**, not yet confirmed in the world. Check the nearby terrain when you arrive. Cave coordinates identify a biome at the stated Y level, not a surface entrance. The Nether uses its own coordinates.

**Suggested route:** Start with the village, iron gear, and shipwreck. Try the nearby Trial Chamber, then pick a forest, archaeology, cave, or ocean outing between the main milestones. The [progression guide](survival-progression.md) places the mansion, monument, stronghold, and harder challenges when their rewards are most useful.
