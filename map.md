# Mansion Island: where to explore

Our Minecraft Java 26.3 world uses seed **`4358921489363102297`**. You start on a small island beside a woodland mansion and village. The community [seed discovery](https://www.reddit.com/r/minecraftseeds/comments/1wt81vg/java_insane_spawn_island_woodland_mansion_crushes/) describes a lush cave and several underground structures nearby.

[Open the interactive seed map](https://mcseedmap.net/26.3.0-Java/4358921489363102297#x=0&z=0&l=0)

Coordinates are **X, Z**. The structure and biome positions below were checked with the server's own locate commands. Underground locations may need some digging; the listed X and Z are not surface entrances.

| Place | X, Z | Why visit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Spawn island | **30, 70** | Starting point |
| Woodland mansion | **64, 96** | Explore the illager-filled mansion beside spawn |
| Taiga village | **48, 96** | Find the village right beside the mansion |
| Lush caves | **16, 80** | Underground biome around **Y 15**; look for the cave opening near spawn |
| Mineshafts | **128, 160** and **−192, 288** | Two nearby underground explorations |
| Trial chambers | **−208, 144** and **336, 112** | Two separate underground challenges |
| Shipwreck | **−256, −208** | Search the ocean northwest of spawn |
| Ruined portal | **−592, 208** | Southwest of the island |
| Cherry grove | **−656, −144** | A scenic trip onto the western mainland |
| Ocean monument | **800, −224** | A bigger underwater challenge to the northeast |
| Ancient city | **−1104, 848** | Deep underground, for a well-equipped expedition |
| Stronghold | **−800, −1600** | The route toward the End |
| Snowy plains | **−2384, −784** | A longer trip if you want snow |

**Suggested route:** Check the village and mansion first, explore the lush cave and mineshafts, then tackle the trial chambers. Save the ocean monument, ancient city, and stronghold for later.
