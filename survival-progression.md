# Minecraft 26.3 — Survival Progression Guide

This is a route for our **Mansion Island** world, not a checklist you must finish in order. Follow the **main path** for the big unlocks; pick a side trip whenever the group wants a change of pace. Coordinates are **X, Z**. See the [world map and location notes](map.md) before setting out, especially for underground and Nether destinations.

**The shape of the journey:** establish the island → try nearby adventures → unlock brewing → clear the mansion → reach the End → choose the harder late-game challenges. The ocean and new 26.3 discoveries are optional trips you can take when their rewards sound useful.

## 1. Settle the island and get equipped

1. **Build a shared base and reach full iron.** Start at **(30, 70)**, visit the village at **(48, 96)**, and gather food, iron armor, a shield, tools, and a bow. Scout the mansion at **(64, 96)**, but save its evokers for stronger gear.
2. **Take an early boat trip.** Search the shipwreck at **(−256, −208)** for supplies and a buried-treasure map. A seed-map treasure site is at **(−631, 57)**; your shipwreck's map may lead elsewhere. Keep any Heart of the Sea for a later [conduit](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/heart-sea).
3. **Unlock enchanting.** Make a diamond pickaxe and an enchanting table with 15 bookshelves. Useful enchantments on iron and selected diamond gear are enough to move on; a perfect set can wait.
4. **Set up a Mending librarian.** At the village **(48, 96)**, find an unemployed adult villager who has never been traded with. Place a lectern beside them during their working hours and check the enchanted-book trade. If it is not Mending, break and replace the lectern until Mending appears; then make one trade to lock that offer. Buy books with emeralds, apply them to your best tools and armor in an anvil, and gain XP while holding or wearing the item to repair it. Later, do the same for Elytra.
5. **Clear a normal Trial Chamber.** Try **(−208, 144)** with your group, collect key and vault rewards, and note the location for Ominous Trials later. It is underground, so the coordinates are a search area rather than an entrance.

**Base project:** Build a [Copper Golem sorting station](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/updates/copper-age-drop) at your base. Put mixed items in a copper chest and seed nearby ordinary chests with the item types you want it to file. This is a useful early storage upgrade, not a requirement for the next adventure.

## 2. Take discovery trips before the boss rush

These outings work well between equipment upgrades. None blocks the Nether or End.

- **26.3 explorer-map relay — (−960, 224).** Go west to the [Dappled Forest Abandoned Camp](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-java-edition-26-3). Search its **chests and barrels** for an explorer map; it may point to another camp, a warm ocean ruin, an Ancient City, or another structure. Copy the map in a crafting table so the group can follow the same target, then travel toward its marker and explore the destination together. The map and its target are random, so this guide cannot name that target's coordinates in advance. For a fixed second stop, visit the **Forest Abandoned Camp at (−1168, −432)**: from the first camp, travel about **208 blocks west and 656 blocks north**, then search its containers for another map.
- **Archaeology — (−1568, 32).** Bring a brush to the [Trail Ruin](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/trail-ruins). Its suspicious gravel offers pottery sherds and armor-trim discoveries. The structure may be partly buried.
- **Night expedition — around (−1548, −626).** Enter the [Pale Garden](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/the-garden-awakens-is-here) after dark, encounter the Creaking, and look for its heart. Enchanted gear and a planned return route make this more fun than stumbling into it unprepared.
- **26.2 cave life — around (852, 6), Y ≈ 0.** Search underground for a [Sulfur Cave and Sulfur Cube](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/updates/chaos-cubed-drop). These are biome coordinates, not a known surface entrance; bring normal caving supplies.
- **Sniffer expedition — (−176, −1520).** Brush suspicious sand at a **warm ocean ruin** for a chance at a [Sniffer egg](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/sniffer). Hatch it back at base, then grow its Torchflower and Pitcher Plant finds. The first ruin may not give an egg; another warm ruin is at **(−576, −1424)**. A boat makes this possible before Elytra; come back after brewing if diving is difficult.

**Travel plan:** The camp, Trail Ruin, and Pale Garden form a western mainland loop. The warm-ocean ruins are a separate northern boat trip. The Sulfur Cave is east of spawn, near the later Ocean Monument route.

## 3. Unlock the Nether, then return for bigger challenges

6. **Find a Nether Fortress and start brewing.** A seed-map fortress is at **Nether (160, −272)**. Bring home blaze rods and Nether wart for potions and Eyes of Ender. Nether coordinates use a different scale from the Overworld; do not treat this as an Overworld waypoint.
7. **Prepare an enchanted diamond combat kit.** Prioritize Protection, Feather Falling, a dependable weapon, and a bow. Upgrade as you play rather than waiting for every ideal enchantment.
8. **Clear the spawn-side Woodland Mansion.** Return to **(64, 96)** for evokers and your first Totems of Undying. A totem is helpful for later fights, though it is not a gate to the Dragon.

### Optional ocean branch after brewing

1. **Raid the Ocean Monument at (800, −224).** Sail northeast from the island with Water Breathing potions, food, and good armor. Dive in, defeat its three Elder Guardians, then collect sponges and prismarine. The [monument guide](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/ocean-monument) explains what you will face.
2. **Build a conduit beside the monument or your ocean base.** Follow the shipwreck's treasure map, or search near the seed-map treasure site **(−631, 57)**, for a Heart of the Sea. Obtain **8 Nautilus Shells** from drowned, fishing, or wandering traders; surround the Heart with the shells in a crafting table. Place the conduit underwater in an open prismarine frame made from your monument loot; the [conduit guide](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/taking-inventory--conduit) shows the frame. This branch can be finished before or after the Dragon.

**Group flight before Elytra:** Search a **Soul Sand Valley** in the Nether, including the area around **Nether (80, 0)**, for a Nether fossil with a Dried Ghast. That coordinate is a search area, **not a confirmed fossil**. Alternatively, craft a Dried Ghast from **1 Soul Sand and 8 Ghast Tears**. Rehydrate and raise it into a [Happy Ghast](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/minecraft-java-edition-1-21-6) for group rides. This is a side project after Nether access, not a prerequisite for the End.

## 4. Reach the End, then choose the harder goals

9. **Defeat the Ender Dragon.** With blaze powder and Ender Pearls, make Eyes of Ender and head toward the stronghold at **(−800, −1600)**. Its portal room is underground.
10. **Find an End City, Shulker Boxes, and Elytra.** After the Dragon, throw an Ender Pearl into one of the small bedrock gateways around the central island to reach the outer End. Bring blocks, spare pearls, food, and Feather Falling boots. Navigate by End **X, Z** toward the [seed-map End City](https://mcseedmap.net/26.3.0-Java/4358921489363102297/end#x=688&z=-864&l=-2) at **(688, −864)**; bridge carefully across void gaps. Kill shulkers there and combine two shells with a chest for a Shulker Box. Look for an **End Ship** beside the city, board it, and take the Elytra from its item frame. If that city has no ship, the next mapped End City to try is **(2560, −928)**. These city locations are seed-map estimates and neither ship has been checked in-game. Add Mending and Unbreaking to the Elytra before regular flight.
11. **Raid a Bastion and start Netherite upgrades.** A seed-map Bastion is at **Nether (80, 0)**. Get a Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template, duplicate it before using the last copy, and upgrade your most useful pieces first. It is close in coordinates, but still a dangerous trip.
12. **Explore the Ancient City for Swift Sneak.** Go to **(−1104, 848)** and use stealth to loot it. Avoiding the Warden is the goal; a full Netherite set is not required.
13. **Return for Ominous Trials and a Mace.** Revisit the chamber at **(−208, 144)** or try **(336, 112)**. The Heavy Core is an ominous-vault reward, so this may take more than one run.
14. **Defeat the Wither and light a beacon.** Farm three Wither Skeleton Skulls, fight in a carefully chosen place, and use the Nether Star for a beacon. A **single 3 × 3 layer of 9 mineral blocks** activates it; a full four-layer pyramid is a later building project, not the first beacon milestone.

**Long-term projects:** Finish your preferred Netherite and enchanted equipment, expand the conduit area into an underwater base, and build a full-power beacon when the minerals come naturally. These are satisfying group goals after the main unlocks, not chores to finish before exploring.
