# Minecraft 26.3 — Survival Progression Walkthrough

A recommended order for a balanced survival playthrough, prioritizing useful upgrades without leaving every optional adventure until you are overpowered.

This is a suggested route, not a list of mandatory prerequisites. When an optional structure is difficult to find, continue with the main progression and return later. Upgrade enough to make the next objective comfortable rather than stopping to perfect every piece of equipment.

1. **Obtain full iron equipment.**

   Obtain iron armor, tools, a shield, and a bow.

   **Why here:** This establishes your first complete adventuring kit. Your next priority is unlocking enchanting rather than immediately grinding for every diamond item.

2. **Obtain a diamond pickaxe and unlock level-30 enchanting.**

   Set up an enchanting table with 15 correctly placed bookshelves and obtain useful mining and combat enchantments.

   **Why here:** Enchanted iron and selected diamond upgrades provide a reasonable foundation for your first major dungeon. You do not need a complete or perfectly enchanted diamond set before continuing.

3. **Complete a normal Trial Chamber.**

   Complete its challenges and collect trial-key and vault rewards. Save Ominous Trials for later.

   **Why here:** Equipment and resource rewards are more useful while you are still assembling your gear. This provides an early adventure before the Nether, but do not stall progression searching for a chamber—return when you locate one.

4. **Conquer a Nether Fortress and unlock potion brewing.**

   Collect blaze rods and Nether wart, then establish a brewing setup.

   **Why here:** Blaze powder supports brewing and crafting Eyes of Ender, advancing both your preparation options and your route to the End. Secure these unlocks before committing to a Bastion or Netherite grind.

5. **Obtain a well-enchanted diamond combat set.**

   Aim for full diamond armor, useful Protection enchantments, Feather Falling, and dependable melee and ranged weapons. Work toward Mending and a reliable experience source alongside this.

   **Why here:** This is a comfortable equipment target for the upcoming combat and End expedition. Useful enchantments matter more than finishing a theoretically perfect set before moving on.

6. **Obtain your first Totems of Undying.**

   Defeat evokers in a raid or an accessible Woodland Mansion.

   **Why here:** Totems provide an additional safety margin for upcoming adventures. This is an optional preparation milestone, not a mandatory gate before the Dragon. A totem must be held to activate, and it will not save you from the void. If obtaining one would require a disproportionate detour, return to this goal later.

7. **Locate the Stronghold and defeat the Ender Dragon.**

   Prepare Eyes of Ender, locate and activate the End portal, and defeat the Dragon.

   **Why here:** Your equipment is now ready for the main boss route. Prioritizing the Dragon before clearing every remaining structure lets you move on to End Cities and their valuable travel and storage rewards.

8. **Obtain Elytra and Shulker Boxes, then establish reliable flight.**

   Find an End Ship for Elytra and collect shulker shells for portable storage. Aim for Mending, Unbreaking III, and a dependable supply of flight rockets.

   **Why here:** Flight and portable storage make longer expeditions and resource collection more convenient. Secure these capabilities before beginning extensive exploration and equipment-upgrade projects.

9. **Raid a Bastion and obtain your first Netherite upgrades.**

   Obtain a Netherite Upgrade Smithing Template, gather ancient debris, and upgrade selected pieces of your main equipment. Preserve a template for duplication.

   **Why here:** This gives your return to the Nether a focused equipment goal. Combine the expedition with Wither preparation where practical, but do not make a complete Netherite set a requirement for continuing.

10. **Defeat the Wither.**

    Collect three Wither Skeleton Skulls and prepare specifically for the fight. Aim for Looting III before deliberately farming skulls, and choose your fighting location carefully.

    **Why here:** The Nether Star unlocks a beacon, giving this boss a practical payoff before lengthy completionist goals. Preparation should account for your edition and difficulty; partial Netherite equipment alone does not guarantee that you are ready.

11. **Activate a full-power beacon.**

    Craft the beacon and complete its four-layer mineral pyramid.

    **Why here:** A working beacon is useful before major excavation and construction projects. However, do not force an excessive mineral grind simply to obey this order—continue adventuring and finish the pyramid when your resource supply makes it reasonable.

12. **Conquer an Ocean Monument.**

    Prepare suitable underwater equipment and potions, defeat the Elder Guardians, and collect sponges and prismarine.

    **Why here:** You already have brewing and strong equipment, making this a natural specialist expedition. This milestone is independent of the Wither and beacon; move it earlier when an underwater project makes its rewards immediately useful.

13. **Clear a Woodland Mansion.**

    Explore and clear a mansion, collecting its loot and additional Totems of Undying from evokers. Count this as complete if you already cleared one earlier.

    **Why here:** Flight makes a distant mansion less of a travel commitment. It is an exploration objective rather than a prerequisite for the next milestone, so take advantage of a convenient mansion whenever you find one.

14. **Loot an Ancient City and obtain Swift Sneak.**

    Explore an Ancient City carefully and recover Swift Sneak books and other valuable loot.

    **Why here:** This adds a stealth-focused challenge and a useful movement enchantment after your main progression unlocks. The objective is to avoid the Warden, not acquire enough armor to fight it. Netherite and a beacon are not prerequisites for this expedition.

15. **Complete Ominous Trials and obtain a Mace.**

    Return to Trial Chambers for their harder challenge. Obtain a Heavy Core from an ominous vault and combine it with a Breeze Rod to craft a Mace.

    **Why here:** This is a substantial optional combat goal once your essential unlocks are established. Keeping the loot-dependent Heavy Core hunt here prevents it from holding up the Dragon, flight, or beacon progression.

16. **Finish your fully upgraded endgame equipment.**

    Complete your Netherite armor and main tools, preferred enchantments, upgraded Mace, and spare adventuring equipment.

    **Why here:** This is the final polishing milestone. Upgrade incrementally throughout the playthrough, then finish your ideal loadout after enjoying the major adventures rather than making perfect equipment a prerequisite for them.

**Guiding rule:** Unlock useful capabilities first, upgrade equipment as needed, and pursue perfection last.
